# Human-only upstream submission authority

OpenHanako coding agents may read and reference public upstream material and prepare local drafts and evidence, but they may never mutate `liliMozi/openhanako`. Human approval confirms draft content only; a human must manually perform any upstream submission outside a Codex or Claude session. This trades automated submission convenience for a durable separation between content preparation and external authority after the repository's earlier wording left comments and other non-creation mutations insufficiently explicit.
